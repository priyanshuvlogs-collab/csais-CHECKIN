# CSAIS Guard Panel — User Guide

This guide is for security guards. The guard panel is the screen you use on
your phone while on duty. It replaces the hourly check-in phone call: instead,
you prove you are awake every 15 minutes with a live camera photo or video.

The same guide is available inside the app: tap **Help** in the top-right
corner of the guard panel.

---

## 1. Register (first time only)

1. Open the CSAIS website on your phone and tap **New guard? Register here**
   (or go to `/register`).
2. Fill in:
   - **Full name** — your first AND last name, exactly as on the company
     roster (example: *John Smith*). One word is rejected.
   - **Mobile phone number** — at least 10 digits. Dispatch calls this number
     if you miss a check-in. You cannot start a shift without it.
   - **Password** — at least 8 characters.
3. Tap **Create account**. You are logged in automatically.

## 2. Log in

1. On the home page tap **GUARD LOGIN**.
2. Enter your **phone number** (or email, if you added one) and your password.

If your profile is incomplete, the panel walks you through the missing steps —
it always shows **only the next thing to do** (Step 1 phone → Step 2 name →
Step 3 start shift).

## 3. Start your shift

When you arrive on post:

1. Type the **site name** where you are posted (example: `Costco`). If the
   site is new, it is saved automatically.
2. Tap the big **START SHIFT** button.

Your start time is recorded automatically, to the second. You now see the
**ON DUTY card**:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Name / Site | Who and where you are |
| Shift started at | Exact start time (h:mm:ss AM/PM, Toronto time) |
| Time on duty | Counts up |
| Next check-in in | Counts down to your next ping |

**Keep this page open and your phone unlocked while on duty.**

## 4. Answer every check-in ping

- The **first ping arrives about 15 seconds** after you start; after that,
  **one ping every 15 minutes**.
- When the orange **CHECK-IN REQUIRED** banner appears, a **TIME LEFT** timer
  counts down from **5:00**. You must send live camera media before it hits 0.

To respond:

1. Allow camera access if your phone asks.
2. Tap **TAKE PHOTO**, or **RECORD VIDEO** (maximum 45 seconds). A short live
   video is the strongest proof that you are awake.
3. Check the preview, then tap **SEND THIS PHOTO / VIDEO**.
4. If the in-page camera does not start, tap the **phone camera app** button —
   it opens your camera directly.

### What is rejected

- Photos or videos **from your gallery** or files saved earlier
- The **same file used twice**
- Videos **longer than 45 seconds**

If your upload is rejected, the message tells you exactly why. Take a **new**
photo or video with the camera and send again — the timer is still running.

## 5. If you miss the 5 minutes

- The check-in is marked **MISSED**.
- **Every dispatch admin is alerted immediately** with your name, site and
  phone number. Expect a phone call — answer it.
- The red banner on your screen confirms dispatch was alerted. The next ping
  still arrives on schedule, so keep going.
- If you upload after the deadline, it is recorded as **LATE** and dispatch is
  told you responded.

## 6. Send your location (optional)

- **Send GPS once** — sends your current position one time.
- **Start live location** — keeps sending your position while you patrol
  (stop it any time).

Dispatch sees your position with a map link. **GPS never replaces the camera
check-in** — the photo/video is always required.

## 7. End your shift

- Tap **END SHIFT** at the bottom and confirm. Pings stop immediately.
- If you forget, the shift **ends automatically after 12 hours**.

## 8. See your own history

Tap **View my check-ins from today** to see every ping from today: when it was
sent, the deadline, when you responded, and the result (OK / LATE / MISSED).

---

### Golden rules

1. Keep the guard panel open and your phone unlocked while on duty.
2. Answer every ping within 5 minutes with a **fresh** camera photo or video.
3. If dispatch calls you, answer the phone.
